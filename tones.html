Tone marks

A mark sits above the first consonant, but the tone it gives depends on that consonant's class.

  • The mark's name isn't the tone's name: ไม้โท gives the high tone on a low-class letter (ค้า kháa), not เสียงโท.
  • ไม้ตรี and ไม้จัตวา only appear on middle-class letters.
  • With no mark, the tone comes from the class, vowel length and final sound. See the tone rules table.
